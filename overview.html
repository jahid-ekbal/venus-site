About VENUS:

VENUS is a competitive gaming organization built to support players, managers, and creators across Bangladesh.

Our goal is to build a sustainable esports ecosystem through leadership, discipline, and professional management.

What We Do:

  • Manage esports teams (PC & Mobile) 🔥
  • Organize daily scrims and tournaments 🏆
  • Provide digital esports services ⚙️
  • Train and develop players 🎯
  • Build a nationwide esports network 🌐
