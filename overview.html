About VENUS:

VENUS is a competitive gaming organization built to support players, managers, and creators across Bangladesh.

Our goal is to build a sustainable esports ecosystem through leadership, discipline, and professional management.

Guild Facilities:

  • 🔥 Manage esports teams (PC & Mobile)
  • 🏆 Organize daily scrims and tournaments
  • ⚙️ Provide digital esports services
  • 🎯 Train and develop players
  • 🌐 Build a nationwide esports network

Leadership Structure:

Chairman – Overall leadership & final decisions

Vice President – Supports leadership & promotions

Managing Director – Daily operations & management

Coach – Squad training & performance

Activity Manager – Activity monitoring

HR Director – Discipline & recruitment

Event Manager*– Event organization
